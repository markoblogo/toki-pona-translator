## Product boundaries

- AI translation is experimental and should not be presented as certified Toki Pona translation.
- `sitelen-layer-plugin` renders Toki Pona; it does not translate source languages.
- `sitelen-emoji` owns the versioned mapping and its aliases.
- `toki-pona-formatting-pass` performs mechanical manuscript QA and formatting; it does not certify literary quality.
